MSc Project - Step 6 Consolidated Results and Charts Summary
Purpose:
  Consolidate Step 3, Step 4, and Step 5 outputs into tables and PNG charts for the dissertation.

Key findings:
  - Clean classification baseline: RandomForest_150 achieved the best BUILDINGID accuracy (0.9991). Building prediction is highly reliable under clean validation conditions.
  - Clean floor baseline: RandomForest_150 achieved the best FLOOR accuracy (0.9136). Floor prediction is more difficult than building prediction, but Random Forest performs strongest.
  - Clean coordinate baseline: KNNRegressor_5_distance achieved the lowest mean positioning error (9.342 m). KNN is the strongest coordinate baseline and becomes the measurement model for filtering.
  - RSSI noise robustness: 10 dBm RSSI noise increased KNN coordinate mean error (10.402 m). Pure RSSI noise caused moderate degradation compared with missing-WAP corruption.
  - Missing-WAP robustness: 30% missing detected WAPs strongly increased KNN coordinate mean error (23.447 m). The model depends heavily on sparse detected access-point signals.
  - Particle filtering under missing data: Particle filtering slightly improved 20% missing-WAP scenario (-0.237 m vs raw). The improvement is small but suggests probabilistic smoothing can help severe missing-signal cases.
  - Filtering limitation: Raw KNN remained best for clean and pure-noise scenarios (No improvement). Filtering is limited because the dataset is not a true continuous trajectory dataset.
